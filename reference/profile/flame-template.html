Where a pass's time goes

GLM-5.3-Flash (IQ2) on nextsycl, an Arc Pro B65 and an Arc Pro B70, measured with device timestamps (NS_PROFILE=gpu). A block's width is its share of the wall time; click one to zoom in.

The draft block (MTP) runs its own attention and experts; those are counted in its row and again inside the MLA and expert rows, so a parent can be a little over its share. Time the GPU spends waiting for the host is counted in the section that runs next (in the prompt, mostly hc pre). In decode the expert section has no inner timestamps: its split into kernels and waiting comes from the grouped kernel measured alone (~0.32 ms a two-row layer pass), marked with stripes.
